Digital Services & Technological Amenities

State-of-the-art technological amenities designed to empower research, digital learning, and independent productivity.

🔍

Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC)

Instant search and reservation access across our entire physical and electronic catalogue, accessible 24/7/365 from any device.

  1. Step 1: Access the OPAC terminal in-library or visit the patron portal.
  2. Step 2: Search by Title, Author, Keyword, or ISBN.
  3. Step 3: Note the Shelf Location Call Number or click "Reserve / Hold".
  4. Timings: Online portal active 24/7; terminal desk assistance 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
📱

E-Books & Digital Media Cloud

Download from a vast library of 25,000+ digital publications, audiobooks, and peer-reviewed journals directly to your e-reader or phone.

  1. Step 1: Authenticate using your Library Card barcode and PIN.
  2. Step 2: Install the partner app (Libby / OverDrive) on your device.
  3. Step 3: Check out up to 4 titles simultaneously with automatic expiration return.
  4. Availability: Instant digital downloads accessible globally 24 hours a day.
🚪

Collaborative Study Suites & Quiet Pods

Acoustically insulated individual focus pods and 6-person group discussion chambers equipped with smart displays and dry-erase walls.

  1. Step 1: Open the room booking schedule on the patron web app.
  2. Step 2: Choose an available time slot (reservable in 2-hour increments).
  3. Step 3: Check in at the information desk 10 minutes prior to slot start.
  4. Timings: Monday through Saturday: 8:30 AM – 8:30 PM.
🖨️

Printing, Photocopying & Digitization

High-resolution color laser printers, book spine scanners, and document binding facilities for thesis and research preparation.

  1. Step 1: Send documents to `print@readershaven.org` or plug in a USB flash drive.
  2. Step 2: Tap your library smartcard at any self-service print terminal.
  3. Pricing: B&W: PKR 5/page | Color: PKR 20/page | High-Res Scanning: 100% Free.
  4. Timings: Operational daily during all open library hours.
📶

High-Speed Enterprise Campus Wi-Fi

Complimentary Gigabit fiber wireless connectivity covering all reading halls, quiet zones, meeting rooms, and courtyard gardens.

  1. Step 1: Connect your device to SSID: ReadersHaven_Free_WiFi.
  2. Step 2: Accept the municipal fair-use agreement on the captive browser portal.
  3. Performance: Symmetric uncapped 100 Mbps throughput per patron device.
  4. Timings: Active continuously during public opening hours.
