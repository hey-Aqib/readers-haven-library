About Readers' Haven

Four decades of free, equitable access to books, knowledge, and community learning for every resident of the city.

📚 Our Story

Readers' Haven Public Library was established in 1984 as a small neighbourhood reading room with fewer than 2,000 donated books. Supported by local educators, students, and families, it grew into the city's central civic library. Today it holds over 50,000 physical volumes and 25,000 digital e-books, and serves more than 12,500 active patrons.

🎯 Our Mission

To cultivate intellectual freedom, champion lifelong learning, and provide open informational, academic, and recreational reading opportunities for every community member, regardless of age, background, or income.

🌟 Our Core Values

Equitable Access

Library services are free to browse and open to all residents. Wheelchair access and quiet reading rooms are available at the central campus.

Lifelong Learning

From the Children's Corner to research labs and study rooms, we support learners at every stage of life.

Community First

Reading circles, student workshops, and public events keep the library a shared and welcoming civic space.

🕰️ Library Milestones

  • 1984

    Readers' Haven opens its first reading room with a donated collection.

  • 2001

    The Central Campus on Knowledge Avenue opens, with a dedicated Children's Corner and reference section.

  • 2015

    The online catalogue and the first digital e-book collection are launched.

  • 2026

    The library website becomes fully containerized, so it can run on any machine with a single Docker command.
