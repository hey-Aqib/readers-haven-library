⚠️ Borrowing Regulations & Late-Return Policies

Readers' Haven is committed to fair sharing of resources among all city residents:

  • Overdue Fines: A standard late-return fee of PKR 25 per book per day applies following a 2-calendar-day grace period.
  • Lost or Damaged Volumes: Assessed at current market replacement value plus a flat PKR 500 conservation and processing fee.
  • Borrowing Status: Account privileges are paused if cumulative overdue fines exceed PKR 500 until cleared at the circulation desk.
  • Online Renewals: Books may be renewed anytime up to 24 hours prior to the return due date provided no hold has been placed by another patron.
